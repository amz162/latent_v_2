   299→  1262→
   300→  1263→2. `StandardScaler().fit_transform(f)` is LEAKY when fit on the FULL per-stock history BEFORE splitting (e.g., train_all_with_backtesting.py:198 train_cnn_lstm, train_cl_tier.py:237 train_gru, train_multi_horizon.py:137 train_lstm, train_remaining_models.py:97,147) but HARMLESS (HARMLESS_PREDICT_TIME) when fit on the most-recent SEQ bars at LIVE-PREDICT time inside a `predict_fn(sdf)` closure (e.g., train_all_with_backtesting.py:178, 224, 269 — the live inference path that doesn't feed back into training).
